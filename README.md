# CS330
Assignments completed during SNHU's CS330 course 

**1. How do I approach designing software?**
  When designing software, I like to start by breaking down the requirements and sketching out a basic plan before jumping into coding. This project helped me improve my ability to structure a 3D scene by considering object placement, lighting, and camera angles beforehand. One new design skill I developed was thinking more critically about how lighting interacts with different materials to create a more realistic scene. 
  For this project, I followed an iterative design process where I started with a simple scene and gradually refined it by adding textures, adjusting lighting, and fine-tuning object placement. I also made use of reference images to guide the design and ensure realistic proportions. This approach helped me work more efficiently and allowed me to catch and fix issues early on.
  Moving forward, I can apply these tactics in future projects by always starting with a clear plan and refining elements step by step. Whether I'm designing software for graphics, AI, or other applications, having a structured process will help me work more efficiently and create polished results. 

  **2. How do I approach developing programs?**
    While working on my 3D scene, I explored new development strategies such as modular coding, where I separated different functionalities like camera movement, lighting, and object rendering. This helped keep my code organized and made it easier to debug specific features without affecting the entire program. 
    Iteration played a huge role in my development process. I started with a basic scene and continuously improved it by testing different textures, tweaking lighting, and adjusting the camera perspective. Each iteration helped refine the visual quality of the scene and ensured that all elements worked together seamlessly.
    Over the course of the project milestones, my approach to coding evolved significantly. At first, I focused more on getting objects into the scene, but as I progressed, I became more mindful of optimizing performance and improving visual quality. By the end, I had a much better understanding of how to balance functionality and efficiency in a 3D environment. 

  **3. How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?**
    Computational graphics and visualizations have given me a strong foundation in 3D modeling, lighting, and rendering, which can be helpful in both my academic and professional pathways. Understanding how to manipulate graphics through code has helped me think more critically about how visual information is processed and displayed. 
    In my future educational pathway, these skills will be valuable if I take courses in game development, computer vision, or AI-driven image processing. The ability to create and manipulate 3D environments will give me a solid technical background for more advanced coursework.
    Professionally, computational graphics could be applied in various fields, from game development to simulations and AI-driven visualizations. Whether I pursue a career in software development, AI, or UI/UX design, having experience with 3D graphics and rendering will set me apart and open opportunities in tech industries that rely on interactive and visual computing.
